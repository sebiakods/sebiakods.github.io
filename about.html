I'm Sebia Kods, an AI Engineer and ML Architect based in El Eulma, Sétif, Algeria. I work across the full ML spectrum — from NLP and computer vision to reinforcement learning and real-time AI systems.

My interest in machine learning started with a simple question: how can a system learn patterns that humans can't explicitly see? That curiosity has driven everything — from building a TF-IDF retrieval engine over 2,000 arXiv papers, to training deep learning models for facial recognition, to deploying forest fire prediction APIs with interactive maps.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science specializing in AI, Image Processing, and Data Mining. My academic path gave me strong foundations in statistical learning, pattern recognition, and distributed systems — which I combine with hands-on engineering to build systems that hold up in production.

When I'm not training models, I'm thinking about how to make them safer, more interpretable, and genuinely useful.

Research

Where I push further

Drug Dosing Optimization in ICU — Offline Reinforcement Learning
Ferhat Abbas University Sétif 1 · Computer Science Lab · Master's Thesis
Jan – Jun 2026

Designed and trained an offline RL system (BCQ & CQL) on 146K clinical transitions from MIMIC-III to personalize insulin dosing. Built a custom MDP with multi-component rewards, clinical feature engineering from raw EHR data, and evaluated policies across 7 axes — OPE estimators, safety metrics, clinician agreement, and subgroup analysis.
