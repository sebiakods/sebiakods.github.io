Research

Where I push further

Drug Dosing Optimization in ICU — Offline Reinforcement Learning
Ferhat Abbas University Sétif 1 · Computer Science Lab · Master's Thesis
Jan – Jun 2026

Designed and trained an offline RL system (BCQ & CQL) on 146K clinical transitions from MIMIC-III to personalize insulin dosing. Built a custom MDP with multi-component rewards, clinical feature engineering from raw EHR data, and evaluated learned policies across 7 axes — OPE estimators, safety metrics, clinician agreement, and subgroup analysis.
